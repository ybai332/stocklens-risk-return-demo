    Methodology

    Annualized returns express observed period returns on a 252-trading-day basis. Annualized volatility is the sample standard deviation of daily returns multiplied by √252. Industry volatility is the average of eligible stocks’ annualized volatilities. Industry counts reflect the number of eligible stocks included in each average.
